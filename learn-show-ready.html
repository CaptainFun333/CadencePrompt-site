Module 1

Your first show

About 8-10 minutes

Doors

This is your first show.
Short, and it goes wrong on purpose.

You are the operator.
Read the cyan lines aloud as the presenter, and run me at the speed you read.

Where it says YOUR TURN, do what it says.
The white lines are for you.

Take your time.
Nobody is watching.

First, before doors.
Open the Show drawer and find Before you go live.
Press Checklist and read it.

Then look at the live bar and the strip above the editor.
The clock is set, and the timer cues are armed when I play.

Your turn Open the Before you go live checklist, then close it.

Show

Doors are open.
Press Play.
The clock starts when I pass the next cue.

Slide - 1: Welcome

Good morning, everyone, and thank you for coming.
My name is Dana, and I will be your host.

Today we are going to look at the year behind us,
and the year ahead.

Audio - Walk-in music out

First, a short story.
Two years ago, a small team set out to build something they could not find anywhere.
They knew it could be better,
so they made it.

Your turn Read the cyan lines aloud and keep up with yourself. Stay on the last word as it passes the marker.

Late change

The producer has a change.
They want the next sentence to read differently.

Change the line below to say: Today, nine hundred people are using it.
Click on it and type, while I scroll.
Edit below the marker, so I do not jump.

Today, three hundred people are using it.
And every one of them started as a skeptic.

Your turn Change three hundred to nine hundred before the line reaches the marker.

Skip

Dana looks at her watch.
She jumps ahead to the closing, and skips two pages.

You do not chase.
Stop me, or take the speed to zero.
Read ahead in the editor and find the line where she landed: Let me finish with this.
Click on it and press Jump here.

This part of the talk is about the money, the plan, and the numbers.
It is long, and she has decided to skip it.

Let me finish with this.
Everything we build starts with someone saying: it does not have to be this way.

Your turn Click on Let me finish with this and press Jump here. Then press Play.

The deck drops

Your Stream Deck just lost its connection.
If you are using one, unplug it now, or imagine it.

I am still moving, at the speed I had.
Stop me with the mouse wheel, or two fingers on the trackpad.

Now start me again, and change my speed with the Speed buttons on the live bar.

Your turn Stop me with the wheel. Start me with Play. Slow me down with the Speed minus button.

Thank you. That is the end of what I wanted to say.
And now, back to the room.

Close

The show is over.
The clock stopped at the Finish cue.

Look at it.
That is how long your first show took.

Run through this list in your head.
I checked the display before doors.
I followed the presenter, and did not lead.
I changed a line while it scrolled.
I found my place after a skip.
I kept the show going when the deck dropped.

If you can say all five, you can run a show.

Your turn Press Top twice, and run it again at a different speed. Try to break it.
