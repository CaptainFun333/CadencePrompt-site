Changelog

Every release, in plain language.

0.3.0 · November 28, 2026

  • Prepare as one tab (Beta): Clean-up works on the open script and on the files waiting in Import & Prep from one tab. Each card has one status line and Scan again, long lists show the rows that need a decision first, and Apply the sure ones can run on every file at once.
  • Tools as tabs: Clean-up, Speaker Style, Pronunciations, New version, the cue sheet, and the Stream Deck layout open as tabs beside Script, in the Extended window and the Mirrored Edit view. Play brings Script forward.
  • Import & Prep in tabs: each import opens as a tab in one window, with keys to step between them and Move to New Window.
  • More than one language (Beta): a Word file with a column or a section per language can become one script per language, Cadence asks about languages before tables, and the import summary says what it left out, like footnotes.
  • More file types: Word .docm, .dotx, and .dotm, PowerPoint .ppsx, and Fountain and Final Draft screenplays.
  • Settings: a ↺ beside any changed setting puts it back in one click, and ⌘Z takes that back. A Defaults window holds your own defaults, and File, then Options, can save your settings and bring them in on another computer.
  • Speaker Style (the old Styles window) finds the names that start each turn, remembers what it applied, and has its own manual page.
  • The bars: the top bar holds the reading controls and the bottom bar holds cues, timer cues, and bookmarks. The speed starts at 0, and its readout is colored by play state (green forward, yellow reverse, red playing at zero).
  • Snap: a landing can take the reading line high while playing and glide back after LAST (Beta), Full dims nothing, and long scripts with many stops scroll more smoothly.
  • Speaker timer: one calm pace that counts only moving time and leaves stage directions out of word counts. +1, −1, and Add change the segment’s target and keep the clock running.
  • Crew: Sign everyone out in Collaboration forgets every crew login, Coming up scrolls with a Now chip, and the shared script no longer jumps on a redraw.
  • Cadence’s own boxes replace most system dialogs, and ⌘K and Help search forgive typos. The Mirrored window gains the NDI settings and spell check in its Edit view, and pressing the Reading line dial puts the line back to your default.

0.2.1 · November 21, 2026

  • Slide pictures at import on a PC (Beta): when PowerPoint is installed, importing a PowerPoint deck with Include slide pictures has PowerPoint draw the slides, and the pictures come in with the cues. Choose a PDF of the slides is only offered if PowerPoint cannot do it.

0.2.0 · November 20, 2026

  • Learn series (new): Cadence now opens on Getting Started the first time you run it. Getting Started (ten modules), Prep Like a Pro (nine modules), and The Whole Room (nine modules) are practice scripts that teach each feature at a stop on the prompter. They have their own Learn pages, and the old Welcome items leave the Help menu.
  • Insert from another script (Beta): open an older script in a read-only viewer beside the one you are editing, then copy lines from it and paste them in. Cues and speaker styles come along, and Cadence asks how to number slides only when they do not already follow on. Works in both the Extended and Mirrored windows, reads Word, PowerPoint, Keynote, Pages, PDF, and Telescript, has its own Find, and can send selected lines to Import & Prep.
  • Stops (Beta): Snap marks are now called stops, drawn as a chevron-and-bar mark in the margin of the prompter and the edit pane. START and LAST mark the ends of a run. Add stop here places a stop by hand on any line.
  • Hold at snap (Beta): playing into a run of snapping stanzas drops the speed to zero and glides the stanza to the top. The wheel and speed dial are ignored for a moment so a hand still turning the speed does not raise it again.
  • Snap keys and buttons (Beta): Snap back and Snap next step one stanza stop at a time, on the top bar and as Stream Deck keys. The top bar buttons read SNAP and are greyed until some line snaps. The Q2Q buttons read Q2Q up and Q2Q down.
  • Search and navigation: ⌘K is one search box for every setting and command, with choices opened in place. The menu bar now mirrors the drawers, NDI moves into the Show drawer, the Stream Deck drawer becomes Controllers, and each settings tray says how far it reaches. A dot marks settings changed from the default.
  • Slide pictures at import (Beta): importing a PowerPoint or Keynote deck can bring in the slide pictures, which show on the prompter too. A slide range cue covers slides that have no script, and Bring in a new version matches slides by number.
  • Prepare: stage directions inside a running sentence go to the review list instead of being colored, a list breaks at its commas, a new After a dash switch breaks at an em dash, and repeated lines can be set the same way in one step.
  • Mark That Moment: one press (⌘⌥J, or a Stream Deck key) stamps the run history when something goes wrong, so you can find it after the show. The run history can be saved as a PDF.
  • Stream Deck: keys are framed in the color of their type, and there is a one-page Plus XL starter. Rewind and Fast Forward show a double chevron when paused. Credit time remaining moves to ⌘⌥G.
  • Crew pages: the shared script marks the line being read, a click on the percentage resets the text size, and the Cue sheet's tick box is now Ready. The Join page says what to check when it cannot connect, and the phone PIN screen is no longer covered by the clocks.
  • Text and fonts: Libre Franklin is bundled and listed in the Font menu, and the Tilt script now uses it. Edit zoom changes only the edit pane. A color highlight keeps its words readable, and Invert keeps a run's own color.
  • Fixes: the Timer count toggle no longer resets the clock. Opening a script clears the old bookmarks from the deck and knob. Opening a runlist now closes the open script first. Menu items like Import & Prep keep their ampersand.

0.1.2 · November 11, 2026

  • Snap to stanza (Beta): a line, or a whole style, can snap, so the page keys stop at stanzas; a stanza taller than the display gets page stops of its own, and Cadence offers to find the best page breaks.
  • Viewer pages (Beta): the Timer, Cue sheet, and Script pages share one navigation, with a phone layout and separate View and Edit PINs.
  • NDI Prompter picture (Beta): Opaque, Transparent, or Green background, with an outline, for a keyer. The prompter display has a Green background too.
  • A key struck twice inside 0.3 seconds now does its job once, for the page keys, cue to cue, and bookmarks.
  • Drawers: the Prompter drawer has folds that remember if they are open, and Network is split into NDI and Collaboration. Help search now points at the control it finds.
  • The Stream Deck page has shorter key captions, new VIEW MARKS, VIEW CUES, and SNAP keys, and a Coming up dial.
  • The Text roll is no longer part of this build.

0.1.1 · October 31, 2026

  • Help, then Report a Problem or Request a Feature, gathers what you tell it with your app and computer details, a fixed list of settings and script sizes, to copy or save as a file. It is in beta builds only.
  • Mark That Moment (Command-Option-J, or Control-Alt-J on Windows) stamps a short record kept in memory, so a report can show when the scroll jumped. Nothing is written to disk.

0.1.0 · October 30, 2026

  • The first beta, for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows: Import & Prep, the live bar, runlists, timer cues, the speaker timer and crew pages, Stream Deck control, and the presenter settings described on the feature pages.
