Changelog

Every release, in plain language.

0.1.2 · November 11, 2026

  • Snap to stanza (Beta): a line, or a whole style, can snap, so the page keys stop at stanzas; a stanza taller than the display gets page stops of its own, and Cadence offers to find the best page breaks.
  • Viewer pages (Beta): the Timer, Cue sheet and Script pages share one navigation, with a phone layout and separate View and Edit PINs.
  • NDI Prompter picture (Beta): Opaque, Transparent or Green background, with an outline, for a keyer. The prompter display has a Green background too.
  • Crawl look (Beta): a tilted crawl with its own panel.
  • A key struck twice inside 0.3 seconds now does its job once, for the page keys, cue to cue and bookmarks.
  • Drawers: the Prompter drawer has folds that remember if they are open, and Network is split into NDI and Collaboration. Help search now points at the control it finds.
  • The Stream Deck page has shorter key captions, new VIEW MARKS, VIEW CUES and SNAP keys and a Coming up dial.
  • The Text roll is no longer part of this build.

0.1.1 · October 31, 2026

  • Help, then Report a Problem or Request a Feature, gathers what you tell it with your app and computer details, a fixed list of settings and script sizes, to copy or save as a file. It is in beta builds only.
  • Mark That Moment (Command-Option-J, or Control-Alt-J on Windows) stamps a short record kept in memory, so a report can show when the scroll jumped. Nothing is written to disk.

0.1.0 · October 30, 2026

  • The first beta, for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows: Import & Prep, the live bar, runlists, timer cues, the speaker timer and crew pages, Stream Deck control and the presenter settings described on the feature pages.

New beta builds are listed here the day they go out.
