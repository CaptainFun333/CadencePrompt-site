Features / A better read · Presenter

The best prompter is the one your presenter never notices.

When the prompter does its job, the presenter connects with the audience instead of the screen. Style, margins, marker and speed are set once per presenter and saved.

Speaker styles

Every speaker in their own style.

Give each speaker a name, a color and a size. Lines pick up the style of whoever is speaking, and you can save your choices as the defaults for new scripts.

SCREENSHOT TO CAPTURECapture: The Speaker styles sheet with three speakers, their swatches and sizes, and the sample preview.

The reading marker

A marker you can see and a line you can follow.

Choose the marker's shape, side, color and size, and where the reading line sits. Save it as your default.

SCREENSHOT TO CAPTURECapture: The Marker and margins sheet showing the marker shape tiles, the swatches and the reading-line setting.

Margins and alignment

Text set where the presenter wants it.

A comfortable normal margin, or your own numbers with a live miniature. Align the script left, centered or right.

SCREENSHOT TO CAPTURECapture: The margins area of the sheet with the live miniature, and the Text tab showing alignment.

Back up smoothly

Go backward as easily as forward.

Turn the speed past zero and the script scrolls backward, so a presenter can repeat a line without a jump.

SCREENSHOT TO CAPTURECapture: The live bar speed control, and a short screen recording of the script scrolling backward.

Pronunciation helperComing soon

Hard names, said right the first time.

Add a phonetic spelling once and it shows with the word in the script, for the names and terms that trip people up.

Per-presenter alignmentComing soon

A different alignment for one presenter.

Align a selection differently without changing the rest of the script.

Try it on your next show.
