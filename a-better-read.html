Features / A better read · Presenter

The best prompter is the one your presenter never notices.

When the prompter does its job, the presenter connects with the audience instead of the screen. Style, margins, marker and speed are set once per presenter and saved.

Features marked Beta are built and tested, but have not yet been tried by hand at a real show. They arrive in the next beta build.

Styles

Every speaker in their own style.

Give each style a name, a color and a size. Lines pick up the style of whoever is speaking, and you can save your choices as the defaults for new scripts.

The reading marker

A marker you can see and a line you can follow.

Choose the marker's shape, side, color and size, and where the reading line sits. Save it as your default.

Margins and alignment

Text set where the presenter wants it.

Tight, Normal or Wide to start, or your own numbers for the left and right margins. Align the script left, centered or right.

Snap to stanzaBeta

Verses and lyrics that move a stanza at a time.

Mark the lines that should snap, by line or by style, and the prompter glides one stanza to the top with the next one dimmed. Long stanzas get page stops, and TURN tags show where to turn.

Back up smoothly

Go backward as easily as forward.

Turn the speed past zero and the script scrolls backward, so a presenter can repeat a line without a jump.

Pronunciation helper

Hard names, said right the first time.

Add a respelling for a name once, and it shows with the word in the script, the first time it appears or every time. Your list is kept, for the names and terms that trip people up.

Per-presenter alignmentComing soon

A different alignment for one presenter.

Align a selection differently without changing the rest of the script.

Try it on your next show.
